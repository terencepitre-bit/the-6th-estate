World / US

Trump Halts Iran Strikes, Says Deal Perimeters Agreed

President Trump canceled planned strikes on Iran, saying Tehran and other Middle Eastern countries asked Washington to hold off on any attack. Trump said the 'perimeters' of a deal had been agreed and that an agreement must be reached 'rapidly.' No further details about the deal's terms or timeline were disclosed.

Why it matters: Whether a deal materializes rapidly — as Trump demands — will determine if the US moves toward or away from military conflict with Iran, with direct consequences for oil prices and regional stability.

Chevron, Exxon, Shell Post Record or Near-Record Profits on War-Driven Oil Prices

Chevron reported its highest quarterly earnings ever, while Shell posted its second-biggest quarter on record. ExxonMobil doubled its earnings compared with the same period last year. All three gains were driven by elevated crude prices tied to wartime market conditions.

Why it matters: Record oil-company profits signal that high fuel prices are not a temporary blip — drivers and households should expect energy costs to remain a significant budget pressure.

Business / Policy

Reddit CEO Questions Google AI Overviews as Stock Falls

Reddit CEO Steve Huffman publicly questioned the value of Google's AI Overviews as Reddit's stock declined, and the company is still weighing whether to renew its content licensing deal with Google. The arrangement had allowed Google to use Reddit data to train its AI systems. Huffman's remarks signal Reddit may walk away from the partnership, putting a significant revenue stream and Google's AI training pipeline at risk.

Why it matters: If Reddit ends its Google deal, the platform may need new revenue sources, which could affect how Reddit operates and what users experience on the site.

Sources: As Reddit stock falls, CEO questions value of Google's AI Overviews
Science / Tech / Health

Engineers Plan Satellite Capture to Save NASA's Swift Mission

Engineers are planning an attempted capture of NASA's Swift satellite as part of an effort to save the mission. The team believes a capture attempt is a viable path forward and described it as 'very much in the cards.' Swift, a NASA observatory, faces an uncertain future without intervention. The capture plan represents the primary strategy engineers have identified to preserve the satellite's operational status.

Why it matters: If the capture succeeds, Swift's continued operation would preserve a key NASA asset used to detect and study high-energy cosmic events like gamma-ray bursts.

Sources: Here's how engineers plan to save the satellite sent to save NASA's Swift mission

Philadelphia First Grader Interviews His Hero, a Real Bird Scientist

NPR connected a Philadelphia first grader with an aspiring interest in science to an adult conservation scientist — the kind of career the child dreams of having. The boy interviewed the bird scientist about his work in conservation science, getting a firsthand look at what the job actually involves.

Why it matters: For parents and educators, the exchange offers a concrete example of how early exposure to working scientists can make conservation careers feel real and reachable to young kids.

Personal Excellence

Nirmal Purja, Who Climbed 14 Peaks in 6 Months, Killed in Pakistan Avalanche

Renowned mountaineer Nirmal Purja was killed in a Pakistan avalanche, his company confirmed. Purja was widely celebrated for scaling all 14 of the world's highest peaks in just over six months in 2019, a feat that cemented his place among the most accomplished high-altitude climbers in history. No further details about the location or circumstances of the avalanche were provided by his company.

Why it matters: Purja's death is a reminder of the extreme and unforgiving risks that even the world's most elite mountaineers face on every expedition.

Sources: Mountaineer Nirmal Purja killed in Pakistan avalanche, his company says
