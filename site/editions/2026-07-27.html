Shein Posts $99M Loss as Trump Tariffs Squeeze Fast Fashion Giant

Shein has swung to a $99 million loss as Trump-era tariffs weigh on sales, a significant reversal for the fast fashion retailer. The financial setback comes as Shein prepares for a stock market debut in Hong Kong. The tariffs, which target goods imported from China, have disrupted the company's low-cost business model that relied heavily on cheap cross-border shipping directly to consumers.

Why it matters: Shoppers who rely on Shein for affordable clothing could see higher prices or reduced product availability as the company absorbs tariff costs ahead of its public listing.

Baltimore Expands 911 to Connect Callers With Social Services Before Crisis Hits

Baltimore is reshaping its 911 system to do more than dispatch emergency responders. Callers whose situations do not require police could instead be connected to social services and healthcare resources, addressing needs before they escalate into full emergencies. The shift reflects a broader rethinking of who responds to which calls, aiming to match residents with appropriate help rather than defaulting to law enforcement.

Why it matters: Residents facing housing instability, mental health challenges, or other non-emergency needs could get connected to support through a number they already know, potentially preventing situations from worsening before professional help arrives.
