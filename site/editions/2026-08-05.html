Trump Killed Digital Equity Act, Then Courts Forced a Partial Revival

The Trump administration eliminated the Digital Equity Act program, but was forced to restore $1.25 billion of it after legal pressure. A judge, however, ruled that a provision targeting race-based digital disparities was unconstitutional, leaving the program partially intact but stripped of that component. The ruling reflects ongoing legal battles over how federal broadband and digital access funding can be structured and to whom it can be directed.

Why it matters: The partial restoration affects funding for internet access programs in underserved communities, but the removal of the race provision limits who those programs can prioritize.

Mid-Coast Maine Residents Fight to Save Miles Hospital Birthing Center

A grassroots coalition in mid-coast Maine is mobilizing to block the proposed closure of the labor and delivery center at Miles Hospital. The community effort centers on preserving local access to birthing services, with residents organizing to oppose the shutdown. No timeline or final decision on the closure has been announced.

Why it matters: If the birthing center closes, pregnant residents in mid-coast Maine would face longer travel times to deliver, raising safety risks for mothers and newborns in a rural region with limited alternatives.

Avalanche in Pakistan Kills Legendary Nepali Climbing Guides

An avalanche in Pakistan killed a group of Nepali mountain guides described as a generation of record-setting climbing legends. Fellow guides said the deaths dealt a devastating blow to Nepal's mountaineering community, wiping out experienced professionals who had set records and trained others in high-altitude work. The loss represents not just individual lives but a deep rupture in the expertise and institutional knowledge that underpins Himalayan climbing expeditions.

Why it matters: The deaths of multiple elite Nepali guides reduces the pool of experienced high-altitude professionals that climbing expeditions worldwide depend on for safety and success.
