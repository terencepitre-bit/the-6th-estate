Oscar-Winning Songwriter Glen Hansard Dies in Crash at 56

Fed Holds Rates Steady Wednesday, But a Hike Remains on the Table

Federal Reserve policymakers met Wednesday to decide on interest rates. The central bank was widely expected to hold rates at their current level. However, officials left open the possibility of a rate increase to address persistent inflation, which has resisted previous policy tightening efforts.

French Court Ruling Opens New Legal Front Against Oil Companies

A French court ruling expanded the options available for corporate accountability lawsuits against oil companies, setting a precedent that legal observers say may influence similar efforts in the United States and other countries. The decision broadens the legal pathways plaintiffs can use to pursue climate-related claims against energy corporations in French courts, and it signals a growing willingness by judiciary bodies to engage with climate liability arguments at the corporate level.

Why it matters: The ruling could shape how American courts and legislators respond to the next wave of climate lawsuits targeting major oil companies.

Fauci Invokes Fifth Amendment, Refuses Senate Committee Questions

Dr. Anthony Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment and refused to answer questions before a Senate committee. Panel chairman Rand Paul has long accused Fauci of funding research that triggered the Covid pandemic and of lying to Congress about it. Fauci denies both charges.

Upstate New York School Halts AI Humanoid Robot Teacher After Backlash

An upstate New York school district paused plans to deploy a humanoid AI-powered robot in classrooms after state education officials, teachers, and local residents raised concerns. The district had planned to use the humanlike robot as a teaching tool before the objections prompted the halt.

Why it matters: The pushback signals that parents, educators, and state officials are drawing early lines around how AI enters their children's classrooms.

Swift Rescue Mission Crippled After Two of Three Reaction Wheels Fail

A preliminary investigation found that two of Link's three reaction wheels are not operable, leaving the Swift rescue mission severely compromised in orbit. Reaction wheels are critical for controlling a spacecraft's orientation and pointing. With only one of three wheels functioning, the mission faces significant operational constraints that put its primary objectives at risk.

Why it matters: Reaction wheel failures can render a spacecraft uncontrollable, jeopardizing any mission objectives Link was launched to accomplish.
