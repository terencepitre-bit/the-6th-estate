Colombia Bans FGM in Landmark Law Protecting Indigenous Girls

Colombia enacted a landmark bill banning female genital mutilation, a practice still carried out by some members of an indigenous group within the country. The law marks a significant legal turning point in a nation where FGM has persisted despite broader efforts to curb it. The legislation was passed amid accounts of the procedure being performed on infants and young girls, in some cases without the knowledge or consent of their mothers.

Why it matters: The new law gives Colombian families and authorities a legal tool to prosecute those who perform FGM, offering direct protection to girls at risk within indigenous communities.

Sources: 'My grandmother subjected my baby to female genital mutilation behind my back'

Italian Waste Workers Rescue Binned €1M Lottery Ticket From Trash

A lottery player in Italy nearly lost a €1 million prize after accidentally throwing away the winning ticket. The winner only realized the mistake after family members pointed out that the numbers he had played for years had finally been drawn. Waste workers helped locate and recover the ticket from the bin. The winner has now claimed the prize.

Why it matters: The story is a reminder to check your lottery tickets before discarding them — a few seconds could be the difference between losing and claiming a life-changing sum.

Sources: Waste workers help winner find binned €1m lottery ticket in Italy
