Trump Signs Executive Orders Targeting Birthright Citizenship

President Trump signed executive orders targeting birthright citizenship, a right guaranteed under the 14th Amendment to children born on U.S. soil. Legal scholar Kim Wehle examined the constitutional ramifications of the orders, which face significant legal challenges given that birthright citizenship has been a settled constitutional principle since the amendment's ratification after the Civil War.

Why it matters: Any successful challenge to birthright citizenship would alter the legal status of children born in the United States to non-citizen parents, affecting millions of families across the country.

Trump Hits Key Chip Material With 15% Tariff to Counter China

The Trump administration imposed a 15% tariff on a key chip material, targeting increasing competition from China's semiconductor industry. The measure is designed to protect US firms operating in the chip sector as Chinese manufacturers expand their capabilities and market presence.

Why it matters: Higher tariffs on chip materials can raise production costs across the electronics supply chain, potentially pushing up prices on devices from smartphones to laptops for American consumers.

Meta Fined $942M Total in Landmark Child Safety Ruling

Meta faces $942 million in total fines after regulators added a $567 million penalty to the $375 million the company was already ordered to pay in the same case. The combined ruling marks the largest child safety fine ever levied against a social media company.

Why it matters: The record penalty signals regulators are willing to impose nine-figure consequences on platforms over child safety failures, raising the stakes for how social media companies handle minors' data and exposure online.

Trump Administration Proposes Shifting Head Start Control to States

The Trump administration proposed new standards for Head Start, the federal early education program serving low-income children. The proposal would give states significantly more control over the program. Child advocacy groups expressed concern over the changes, warning that shifting oversight away from the federal level could weaken protections and consistency for the program's most vulnerable participants.

Why it matters: Families relying on Head Start for affordable early childhood education may face changes in program quality and access depending on how their state chooses to administer the new standards.

Ice Cream Truck Driver Holds World Record for 70-Year Career

A man who has driven an ice cream truck for 70 years holds the world record for the longest career as an ice cream distributor. He reflected on his decades-long career in a conversation recorded for StoryCorps.

Why it matters: His story is a rare portrait of lifelong dedication to a single trade, offering a window into how one person's consistency shaped community life across generations.

UEFA Warns Infantino Boycott Still On After FIFA Backs President

UEFA declared that FIFA's backing of president Gianni Infantino at a Wednesday meeting 'changes nothing,' signaling a boycott of the FIFA Congress remains possible. The Football Association also withdrew its support for Infantino, adding pressure on the FIFA president. The standoff marks a deepening rift between European football's governing body and FIFA leadership over Infantino's continued tenure.

Why it matters: A UEFA-led boycott of FIFA's Congress would fracture global football governance at a critical moment ahead of the 2026 World Cup.
