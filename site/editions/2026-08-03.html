World / US

Iran Denies Trump's Claim of New US Nuclear Talks Monday

Iran denied Trump's assertion that new nuclear negotiations between the two countries are set to begin Monday. Iranian officials disputed that any direct talks are scheduled or underway with the United States. Separately, Iran is currently in negotiations with Oman over access to the Strait of Hormuz, a critical shipping chokepoint through which a significant share of global oil supplies passes.

Why it matters: Conflicting signals over whether US-Iran nuclear talks are actually happening leave energy markets and US foreign policy in uncertainty, with Strait of Hormuz access negotiations adding a direct pressure point on global oil prices.

Sources: Iran denies Trump's claim that new talks will begin on Monday
Money & Markets

US and Japan Intervene Jointly to Prop Up Falling Yen

The United States and Japan took joint action to support the yen in a rare coordinated currency intervention. Both countries stated they will not hesitate to conduct similar joint interventions in the future.

Why it matters: A stronger yen affects the cost of Japanese imports and exports, with ripple effects on global trade prices and US consumers who buy Japanese goods.

Sources: US and Japan take action to prop up yen in rare joint move
Business / Policy

Capital One Closed Trump Org Accounts Over Money-Laundering Review, Not Jan. 6

Capital One told a judge it shut down hundreds of Trump Organization accounts in 2021 following a monthslong internal money-laundering review — not in response to the January 6 Capitol riot. The bank is seeking permanent dismissal of a lawsuit filed by Trump-affiliated businesses, which had challenged the account closures. Capital One argues the review, not political motivation, drove the decision.

Why it matters: The outcome could set a precedent for when and why banks can close business accounts without facing legal liability from account holders.

Sources: Capital One says it closed Trump Organization accounts over money-laundering concerns
Science / Tech / Health

Veterinarians Begin Making Hive Calls as Honeybee Health Declines

Veterinarians are increasingly taking on honeybee patients as the vital pollinators face growing threats. The move marks a shift in how animal medicine approaches insects critical to agriculture. Bees pollinate a significant share of the food supply, making their health a practical concern well beyond beekeepers. Vets are described as gingerly embracing the specialty, reflecting both the novelty and the urgency of treating hive-based patients at scale.

Why it matters: Honeybee health directly affects the availability and cost of fruits, vegetables, and nuts that depend on pollination to reach grocery shelves.

Sources: Meet the Bee Vet, Now Making Hive Calls
Education

440 Private Colleges At Risk as Campus Closings Accelerate

More than 440 private, nonprofit four-year colleges and universities nationwide are now at risk of closing, driven by falling enrollment, rising debt, and related financial pressures. The pace of closings is accelerating, producing a sudden secondary market in abandoned campuses. Burlington College in Vermont, which closed in 2016, stands as an early example of the trend now spreading more broadly across higher education.

Why it matters: If your family is weighing a private college, the institution's financial stability is now a real variable — students caught in a closure can lose credits, financial aid, and academic momentum mid-degree.

Sources: Pace of college closings picks up, with more projected
Personal Excellence

AI App Accelerates Criminal Record Expungement for Millions of Americans

Tens of millions of Americans carry criminal records that block access to employment. A new AI-powered app is helping eligible individuals expunge those records at a pace not previously seen. The tool streamlines a process that has historically been slow, costly, and difficult to navigate without legal help, opening a faster path for people who qualify to clear their records and reenter the workforce.

Why it matters: Expunging a criminal record can remove a major barrier to getting hired, meaning this technology could directly expand job access for millions of Americans.

Sources: This lawyer believes in second chances. Now AI is providing a boost
